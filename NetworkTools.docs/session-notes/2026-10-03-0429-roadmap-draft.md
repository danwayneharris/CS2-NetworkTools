## 04:59 - clarify NT-021 displacement semantics

Dan clarified that the solver chooses the junction elevation; the user limits
its displacement from the original height. Updated the roadmap to replace
ambiguous strength/blending language with a constrained profile solve. This is
not a user-assigned elevation pin. Disabled/zero preserves original height;
nonzero allowances constrain movement in either direction. Suggested meter-based
label, range/default/persistence and upper-end behavior remain planning details.
This supersedes the earlier note leaving partial-strength meaning unresolved.
Priority remains second; changes remain uncommitted for review.
